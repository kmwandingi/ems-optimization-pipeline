\toprule 
Agent & Role & Key Interactions \\
\midrule 
Global Optimizer & Central decision-making unit. Solves a master optimization problem to coordinate all assets and minimize system-wide costs. & Receives constraints and forecasts from all agents; sends optimal schedules and control signals back to them. \\
Probability Model Agent & Learns and predicts user behavior patterns for flexible devices from historical data. Provides probabilistic forecasts for device usage. & Consumes historical usage data; provides start-hour probability distributions to Flexible Device agents. \\
Flexible Device Agent & Models the operational constraints and user preferences for a single deferrable appliance (e.g., washing machine, dishwasher). & Receives start-hour probabilities; provides a flexibility model (energy, duration, constraints) to the Global Optimizer. \\
Battery Agent & Manages the state of the battery energy storage system (BESS), including state of charge (SoC), charge/discharge limits, and efficiency. & Provides its current state and operational constraints to the Global Optimizer; receives and executes the optimal charge/discharge schedule. \\
EV Agent & Manages the electric vehicle charging process, considering target SoC, departure time, and charging constraints. & Provides its state and charging requirements to the Global Optimizer; receives and executes the optimal charging schedule. \\
PV Agent & Forecasts solar power generation based on weather data (e.g., solar irradiance) and PV panel specifications. & Consumes weather forecasts; provides the predicted PV generation profile to the Global Optimizer. \\
Grid Agent & Represents the connection to the utility grid. Manages electricity tariffs (import/export prices) and grid capacity constraints. & Provides price signals and grid constraints to the Global Optimizer; executes the final grid import/export plan. \\
